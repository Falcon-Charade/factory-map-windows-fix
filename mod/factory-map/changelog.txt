Version: 0.4.0
Date: 2026-11-14
  Features:
    - Live map: the website map now keeps itself up to date while people play, a little at a time, instead of
      capturing the whole map every few hours. Building, mining, landfill and destroyed buildings show up
      within a minute or two; a slow background check catches ore running down, trees and biters spreading.
    - /map-refresh re-reads the whole map at once (/map-snapshot still works and does the same).
    - Power: the main network's generating capacity (steady and solar) for the website.
  Changes:
    - No more short freeze when a snapshot started: explored chunks are tracked as they get explored.
    - Settings: "snapshot interval" is replaced by "re-check everything every (minutes)".
    - Building counts come straight from the game (exact, and no longer wait for a snapshot).
    - Trains and vehicles are no longer drawn into the map picture (they move; the website shows them live).
